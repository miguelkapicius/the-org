# Case studies: indie management, esports-management and sports-management games on Steam, and their marketing

Research date: 2026-11-09. Every sales, wishlist and revenue number here is a **third-party ESTIMATE** (Raijin, Gamalytic, SteamData.AI, Datahumble) unless marked otherwise. Different trackers often disagree by 2x or more, and those gaps are flagged below.

## 1. Which esports-management games exist on Steam, how were they received, how much did they sell, and how were they marketed?

### Takeaway
The CS-like esports manager niche is real and has recently been proven. **Esports Manager 2026** (Neurona Games / indie.io, July 2026, $19.99) is the clearest direct comparable: an estimated 124K–274K copies sold, an all-time peak of about 10K CCU and about 134K+ estimated wishlists. It got there **despite Mixed launch reviews**, and it did it with licensed real CS orgs and players, community figures (casters and coaches) and a demo shown at Steam Sports Fest and Next Fest. A smaller CS rival (CS Manager, 2D) is **Very Positive** but sold only about 3K copies (estimate) with about 10.7K wishlists. MOBA-themed managers (Teamfight Manager 1 and 2, Esports Godfather) show the wider "esports manager" audience is worth roughly 100K+ copies per hit.

### Cited Findings
**Esports Manager 2026 (EM2026): the main direct competitor, CS2-themed**
- Developed by Neurona Games and published by indie.io. Released 6 July 2026. The base price shown was R$59.99 (BRL store), and Raijin lists $19.99 USD. Tags include eSports, Management, 2D, Top-Down and Realistic. Features include a real-time Simulation Mode, transfers, training, a "Talk" module, staff and sponsors, and "real teams and real players" — [Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2749950/Esports_Manager_2026/)
- Steam reviews: **all-English 67% (Mixed, 875 reviews)**, and **recent 81% Very Positive (329 reviews in 30 days)**. Reception improved after patches — [Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2749950/Esports_Manager_2026/)
- Raijin estimates: **about 124K copies, about $1.49M gross, about 134K wishlists (137.6K listed for November), 15,079 followers, an all-time peak of 10,062 CCU (July 2026)**, and 73% positive across 4,784 reviews (all languages). The publisher is indie.io — [Raijin](https://raijin.gg/app/2749950/Esports_Manager_2026)
- Gamalytic estimates **274.1K copies (range 179.9K–368.4K), about $3.6M gross**. This is about 2x Raijin's figure, so treat both numbers as rough — [Gamalytic, via search snippet](https://gamalytic.com/game/2749950) (the direct fetch returned HTTP 429)
- One source says the game launched with **more than 180K wishlists**. This is unverified and came from a single search snippet — [search results around esportsmanager.gg / press](https://esportsmanager.gg/)
- **Marketing timeline:** the first public demo debuted during **Steam Sports Fest on 8 December 2025** (about 70% of systems, one in-game month). Promotion pushed the Discord, X (@EManagerGame) and the publisher's account. The release names CS community figures who appear in the game (casters, coaches and pros such as Ne0Kai, BanKs, PiNGCasts and bit1) and real orgs (Eternal Fire, Copenhagen Wolves, M80, GamerLegion) — [DLH.net](https://www.dlh.net/en/news/87275/esports-manager-2026-debuts-first-demo-during-steam-sports-fest.html); [GamesPress](https://www.gamespress.com/Esports-Manager-2026-Debuts-First-Demo-During-Steam-Sports-Fest)
- An updated demo shipped for **Steam Next Fest (February 2026)** with 250+ predefined tactics on seven maps and a reworked Tactic Creator. The game also appeared at an "Indie Fan Fest" — [FixGaming](https://www.fixgamingchannel.com/esports-manager-2026-steam-next-fest-demo-update/); [GamesPress](https://www.gamespress.com/Esports-Manager-2026-Scouts-Career-Defining-Talent-at-Indie-Fan-Fest)
- Inven Global: the project started in 2019 as "Esports Manager: MOBA", which was scrapped. It licenses FaZe, NAVI, GamerLegion, Eternal Fire and Copenhagen Wolves, plus players such as ZywOo and s1mple, and includes caster lines (Affinity, Boggs). It has a community database for editing rosters. **The first patch arrived one day after launch and fixed 25+ issues.** Criticisms: AI-generated faces and logos hurt authenticity, there are no lower-tier tournaments, and **match viewing has no highlights or replays, which weakens it as a spectator experience** — [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/blackdesertonline/articles/23674/creating-the-football-manager-of-esports-esports-manager-2026)

**CS Manager: a small direct CS competitor**
- Made by Crumana and released 6 August 2026 at $24.99. Rated **Very Positive (82–83% of about 94–100 reviews)**. It is 2D, and matches can be watched on 8 maps with real-time timeouts and instructions. It supports Steam Workshop and is sold in an "Indie Manager Bundle" with the developer's Horse Racing Manager — [Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3418500/CS_Manager/)
- Raijin estimates: **about 2.9K copies, about $44K gross, about 10.7K wishlists, 1,303 followers, an all-time peak of 105 CCU** — [Raijin](https://raijin.gg/app/3418500/CS_Manager)
- Reviews praise roster depth and role thinking and say the developer is very responsive on Discord. Bugs are reported — [Steam reviews (search summary)](https://steamcommunity.com/app/3418500)

**Esports Team Manager (The Brain Trust, December 2025, $9.99): a failure case**
- Raijin estimates about 114 copies, about $911 gross, about 1.3K wishlists, 158 followers, an all-time peak of 10 CCU and 40% positive from 5 reviews — [Raijin](https://raijin.gg/app/3026430/Esports_Team_Manager)

**ESport Manager (2019): an older failure case**
- Poorly received. One review said it had "little to no redeeming qualities" — [Steam reviews](https://steamcommunity.com/app/652660/reviews/?p=1&browsefilter=toprated)

**Teamfight Manager 1 and 2 (Team Samoyed, MOBA-themed, pixel art)**
- TFM1 sells for $9.99. Raijin estimates **about 131K copies and about $1.1M**, while SteamData.AI estimates **about 276K copies and about $2.0M**, a 2x disagreement — [Raijin](https://raijin.gg/app/1372810/Teamfight_Manager); [SteamData.AI (via search)](https://steamdata.ai/game/1372810)
- TFM2 entered Early Access on about 25–26 May 2026. Reviews are **Mixed (about 60% of about 2,040 reviews in early July 2026)**, and the all-time peak was about 5–6K CCU (trackers disagree). Raijin estimates about 58K wishlists. The launch trailer featured recognizable esports personalities (IgNar, Mowgli), and the game has Steam Workshop support for champions, maps, teams and tournaments — [White Market news](https://news.white.market/latest/teamfight-manager-2-esports-sim-steam-early-access/); [Raijin](https://raijin.gg/app/3009300/Teamfight_Manager_2); [Esports News UK](https://esports-news.co.uk/?p=195558)

**Esports Godfather (MOBA-themed, a management deckbuilder)**
- Rated about 90–91% positive across about 4.5K reviews. Raijin estimates **about 99K copies and about $1.2M**. The SteamDB all-time peak was 5,183 CCU (25 May 2024). It has had DLC expansions (January 2025 and October 2025) and a sequel or spinoff ("The Legend Begins") — [Raijin](https://raijin.gg/app/1278540/Esports_Godfather); [SteamDB](https://steamdb.info/app/1278540/charts); [Datahumble](https://datahumble.com/esports-godfather-the-legend-begins-2960430)

**Other titles and fan projects**
- MOBA Esport Manager 25 (Manager H) is "coming soon" — [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2349840/MOBA_Esport_Manager_25/)
- Fan and free projects: **Global Esports Manager** (itch.io, an FM-style CS2 game built with AI-assisted tools, in development), **LIGA Esports Manager** (a free tool that runs real CS:S and older CS **bot matches on a local server**), and **CS Legend** (a browser career sim with round-by-round watchable matches) — [itch cs2man](https://cs2man.itch.io/cs2man); [itch LIGA](https://lemonpole.itch.io/liga); [itch CS Legend](https://cs-legend.itch.io/cs-legend)

### Inferences
- **The CS-manager demand is proven**: EM2026 reached about 10K peak CCU and six figures of sales (estimated) even with Mixed reviews. CS Manager, though reviewed well, stayed small (about 3K copies, estimated). The gap points to **reach/marketing (publisher, licensed real orgs and pros, creator involvement, fest demos)** as the main difference, not quality.
- EM2026's levers were a publisher (indie.io), real licensed teams and players, CS caster and coach cameos, multiple fest demos (Sports Fest, then Next Fest) and community roster editing. A 2-person own-IP team cannot copy the licensing. It **can** copy the community figure and caster cameos (cheaper than licenses), the multi-fest demos and moddable rosters (Workshop or a community DB), so players can recreate real-scene teams themselves.
- Direct-competitor weak spots our game can attack: **2D top-down match viewing with no highlights or replays** (EM2026), match AI that looks dumb (chasing kills instead of planting), arbitrary player ratings, AI-generated art, and a launch shipped too early.
- The sizes are roughly comparable to MOBA-manager hits (TFM1 about 130–275K, Godfather about 100K, both estimates), so the realistic upside for a well-marketed esports manager is low-to-mid six figures of copies.

### Gaps
- No verified pre-launch wishlist numbers from any of these developers. The ">180K wishlists" figure for EM2026 comes from one unverified snippet.
- "Esports Life Tycoon" and a Valorant-specific Steam manager could not be confirmed to exist.
- No public postmortem from Neurona, Crumana or Team Samoyed on marketing spend or channels was found.
- SteamDB tag charts (eSports tag) were blocked (403), and Gamalytic was rate-limited (429).

## 2. Which indie sports-management games succeeded, and how did they grow wishlists (demos, Next Fest, Reddit, streamers, community, early access)?

### Takeaway
Little hard data exists from indie sports-management postmortems. The evidence that does exist points to demos timed to themed fests (Sports Fest) and Next Fest, niche communities that reward depth (Football Coach: College Dynasty, about 95% positive), and sequels and franchises. Next Fest amplifies existing hype rather than creating it.

### Cited Findings
- **Football Coach: College Dynasty** (Wolverine Studios, $19.99): **Overwhelmingly Positive, about 95% of about 1.4–1.5K reviews**. SteamData.AI estimates **about 46K units and about $591K**. The SteamDB all-time peak was 647 CCU (January 2025). Datahumble forecasts about 17.5K wishlists. This is a niche, deep sim that sold steadily without a big CCU spike — [SteamData.AI](https://steamdata.ai/en-US/game/2151290/football-coach-college-dynasty); [SteamDB](https://steamdb.info/app/2151290/charts); [Datahumble](https://datahumble.com/football-coach-college-dynasty-2151290)
- **Simple Football League Manager** (a solo Godot developer) released a free demo before Next Fest, with a launch set for 3 December 2026. Its positioning is "cozy" management without micromanagement — [EIN Presswire](https://www.einpresswire.com/article/945482089/indie-game-simple-football-league-manager-brings-a-cozy-approach-to-football-management-demo-now-available-on-steam)
- GameDiscoverCo on October 2026 Next Fest: the **median top-10 gain was +80K wishlists (down from +143K in June)**, and games ranked #11–30 gained about +30–50K. There were **more than 7,300 demos (up 66.6%)**. "Next Fest mostly amplifies existing hype rather than creating it": pre-fest demo momentum was key, and developers are moving big demo reveals outside the event. GameDiscoverCo also recommends setting a release date 60+ days ahead so the game shows in Steam's Personal Calendar — [GameDiscoverCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/who-won-october-2026s-steam-next)
- Football Manager 2027 was #10 on GameDiscoverCo's trending-wishlists list in October 2026, ahead of a free pre-launch demo. This signals that the genre leader itself uses demos — [GameDiscoverCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/who-won-october-2026s-steam-next)
- EM2026 debuted its demo in **Steam Sports Fest** (December 2025) and then updated it for **Next Fest** (February 2026). This two-fest pattern landed in the release window — [DLH.net](https://www.dlh.net/en/news/87275/esports-manager-2026-debuts-first-demo-during-steam-sports-fest.html); [FixGaming](https://www.fixgamingchannel.com/esports-manager-2026-steam-next-fest-demo-update/)
- Crumana bundles its titles (CS Manager with Horse Racing Manager) as an "Indie Manager Bundle", cross-promoting to its own management audience — [Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3418500/CS_Manager/)

### Inferences
- For us: launch the demo early (outside Next Fest, for example in a themed **Sports Fest** or an esports-adjacent fest) to build momentum, then use Next Fest as an amplifier. Expect a smaller Next Fest bump in late 2026 than in earlier years.
- Deep, niche sims (Football Coach: College Dynasty) can earn great reviews and a long tail with modest wishlists, but reaching Popular Upcoming needs a concentrated spike (fests, creators).

### Gaps
- No primary postmortems with wishlist curves were found for indie sports managers (for example Motorsport Manager, Franchise Hockey Manager, Cricket Captain, or basketball and hockey managers). Pre-2022 cases were not researched because of scope and tool-call limits.
- No data was found on Reddit-driven wishlist spikes for manager games specifically.

## 3. What do players want from an esports manager, and which complaints hurt launches?

### Takeaway
Players want **FM-level depth plus authenticity** (real-feeling scene, sensible ratings, believable match AI), and they want matches **worth watching**. Launch-killers are a barebones or "empty after a few seasons" state, bugs, dumb match AI, arbitrary ratings, AI-generated art, and no highlights or replays.

### Cited Findings
- EM2026 reviews: "VERY barebones, and borderline broken" and "feels empty once you have played a few seasons", while others call the management systems "genuinely addictive" — [Steam reviews](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2749950/reviews/?browsefilter=toprated); [discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2749950/discussions/0/585055193116421711/)
- Gamohol (2.5 score): match AI chases kills instead of planting with time left, defuse logic breaks down, maps feel uneven, famous players don't match their reputations, IGL ratings are "arbitrary", morale spirals after one loss, real names need community content, and the game "shipped a year or two ahead of when it was actually ready". Praised for scouting, contracts, training and staff "good bones" — [Gamohol](https://gamohol.com/esports-manager-2026-game-review/)
- Inven: no highlights or replays, AI faces and logos hurt authenticity, and there are no lower-tier tournaments or unexpected events — [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/blackdesertonline/articles/23674/creating-the-football-manager-of-esports-esports-manager-2026)
- A search-summarized review note on EM2026: it is hard to find a reason to keep watching the matches — [search summary referencing EM2026 coverage](https://www.invenglobal.com/blackdesertonline/articles/23674/creating-the-football-manager-of-esports-esports-manager-2026)
- CS Manager reviewers value role-aware roster building and an active developer on Discord — [Steam community](https://steamcommunity.com/app/3418500)
- TFM2 Early Access is Mixed (about 60%) compared with the well-liked TFM1 (about 83%). Sequel expectations can hurt an EA launch — [White Market](https://news.white.market/latest/teamfight-manager-2-esports-sim-steam-early-access/); [Raijin](https://raijin.gg/app/3009300/Teamfight_Manager_2)
- EM2026's recent reviews rose to 81% after patches, from 67% overall — [Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2749950/Esports_Manager_2026/)

### Inferences
- Our "pro-like bots" and "watchable 3D broadcast" pillars directly answer the most cited weaknesses of the market leader (dumb AI, unwatchable 2D matches, no highlights). This should be the marketing headline.
- Own-IP removes licensing complaints, but expect players to want a real-scene feel. **Roster editing / Workshop** is the cheap answer, and both CS Manager and TFM2 ship Workshop.
- Avoid AI-generated art in public-facing assets, because it was explicitly criticized.
- Polish the demo and launch: Mixed launch reviews are survivable with a big audience, but for a 2-person team without a publisher they probably are not.

### Gaps
- No Reddit threads (r/GlobalOffensive, r/ManagementGames) were retrieved directly. The player-wants evidence comes from Steam reviews and press.

## 4. Is there evidence that a "watch the match in 3D like a broadcast" hook or AI pro-like bots resonate?

### Takeaway
There is no direct evidence (viral clips or data) for a 3D broadcast CS manager, because **none appears to exist on Steam**. Every current CS manager is 2D or top-down. Indirect evidence suggests a gap: reviewers say EM2026's 2D matches lack spectator appeal, highlights and replays, and fan projects such as LIGA (real CS bot matches) and CS Legend (watchable rounds) show demand for watching actual CS-like play.

### Cited Findings
- EM2026 is tagged 2D and Top-Down, and CS Manager is 2D — [EM2026 Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2749950/Esports_Manager_2026/); [CS Manager Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3418500/CS_Manager/)
- "Match viewing lacks highlights or replays, weakening its appeal as a spectator experience" (paraphrased from Inven) — [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/blackdesertonline/articles/23674/creating-the-football-manager-of-esports-esports-manager-2026)
- LIGA Esports Manager runs manager-driven matches as real CS bot games on a local server, a fan-made attempt at "watchable real matches" — [itch.io LIGA](https://lemonpole.itch.io/liga)
- No commercial 3D, FM-scale CS manager with fully watchable 3D matches was found — [search summary covering itch/Steam](https://cs2man.itch.io/cs2man)

### Inferences
- 3D broadcast-style matches are a **clear visual differentiator** for capsule art, trailers and shorts. GIF-able clutch moments with caster-style overlays are the kind of asset that reaches CS audiences, but this is unproven. A/B test short clips (TikTok, Shorts, r/GlobalOffensive) before committing the whole pitch.
- Our bots may make "smart" plays (trades, retakes, utility). Clips showing them should be framed against the known complaint that "AI chases kills instead of planting".

### Gaps
- No viral-clip metrics were found for any manager game's match footage. Data on CCU or wishlist lift from match-footage trailers was not available.

The esports-manager niche supports a few roughly 100K-copy hits. Top CCU peaks run from about 5K to 10K, and followers for the leader are about 15K. Followers convert at roughly a 1:9 ratio to wishlists for EM2026 (15K followers to about 134K wishlists, estimated).

### Inferences
- The CS fanbase is huge, and EM2026 proved that a CS-themed manager can hit about 10K CCU. The 2D-vs-3D gap and the AI-quality complaints leave room for a second strong entrant, but EM2026 now holds the "Football Manager of CS" mindshare, so our positioning must be differentiated ("watch your team play like a real broadcast").
- CS Manager shows what happens with no publisher, no licenses, no creator push and no visual hook: good reviews but about 3K copies. That is our downside scenario if marketing is neglected.
